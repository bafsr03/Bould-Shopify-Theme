# Bould

Bould is a retail technology company powering the digital infrastructure that modern eCommerce and retail brands need to thrive. From high-ranking, conversion-optimized websites to custom web development, immersive 3D stores, and cutting-edge virtual try-on technology, Bould delivers end-to-end solutions that elevate the online shopping experience. With seamless Shopify integration, Bould makes it effortless for brands to scale, sell, and stand out in today's competitive digital landscape.
